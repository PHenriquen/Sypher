# Endorsement and naming rules

## Parent → division

Use the division name when the domain matters:

- Sypher Intelligence
- Sypher Products
- Sypher Systems
- Sypher Engineering
- Sypher Interactive
- Sypher Labs

Do not invent new divisions for every project. A new division is justified only when multiple products or a materially different capability cannot fit the existing architecture.

## Division → product

Preferred technical/portfolio lockup:

`PROJECT — SYPHER DIVISION`

Examples:

- `ILLUME — SYPHER INTELLIGENCE`
- `NODI — SYPHER PRODUCTS`
- `SINCROHUB — SYPHER SYSTEMS`
- `RÉQUIEM — SYPHER INTERACTIVE`

Preferred lightweight marketing lockup:

`PROJECT — BY SYPHER`

The product logo must remain visually dominant. Sypher is an endorsement, not a prefix that replaces the product identity.

## Labs graduation

An experiment starts in Labs when its product/customer/category is not yet proven. When it becomes a real software product, system, device or game, move it to the appropriate division. Keep the project history; do not erase the Labs origin.

## Group

Reserve **Sypher Group** until there is a genuine holding/group reason for it. It is not a prestige suffix.

## Founder / After Hours

Founder portfolio, drawings, streaming and creator channels are personal. They can be linked from the corporate website, but they should be labeled as personal and must not appear as company divisions or company products.
