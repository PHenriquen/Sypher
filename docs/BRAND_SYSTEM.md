# Sypher brand system — v1 working foundation

This document defines the brand architecture, visual grammar and endorsement rules for the current Sypher working identity. The architecture is intentionally more durable than the parent name: if the parent name changes, the division logic, project lineage and most design tokens should survive.

## 1. Brand architecture

Sypher is the parent brand. It is not a software house category and it should not be visually trapped inside AI, industrial automation, gaming or hardware. The parent identity stays neutral and the divisions carry domain-specific expression.

### Active divisions

- **Sypher Labs** — research, prototypes and experimental R&D.
- **Sypher Intelligence** — AI, assistants, agents and intelligent automation.
- **Sypher Products** — SaaS and general digital products.
- **Sypher Systems** — industrial and operational software.
- **Sypher Engineering** — hardware, IoT, embedded and physical systems.
- **Sypher Interactive** — games and digital experiences.

### Reserved structure

**Sypher Group** is reserved for a future holding/conglomerate structure. It should not be used while Sypher is one operating company with internal divisions. Using “Group” too early makes the company look larger on paper than it is in reality.

## 3. Master visual grammar

The reference video favors compact geometric marks, dark presentation fields, strong center geometry, controlled glow, rings/cores, angular modules and single bright accents. The system distills those traits instead of reproducing any reference logo.

### Shared rules

1. **64 × 64 construction grid.** Marks are designed to survive app-icon and favicon scale.
2. **One dominant gesture.** A mark should be understandable as a silhouette before effects are added.
3. **Dark-first, not dark-only.** Every mark must also work in one-color black and one-color white.
4. **One accent per division.** Gradients are secondary presentation effects, never required to identify the mark.
5. **Chambered geometry.** Cuts, gaps and negative spaces are preferred over decorative lines.
6. **No generic AI shorthand.** Avoid brains, robot faces, chat bubbles, microphones, literal atoms and a standalone four-point sparkle.
7. **Effects are UI, not logo.** Glow, particles and blur can appear in motion or web presentation but are not part of the core vector mark.

## 4. Parent identity

### Working mark: Cipher Ribbon

The parent mark uses two opposing angular ribbons that create an abstract **S** while a central diamond cut behaves like a key/cipher interruption. It is intentionally monochrome so it can sit above all six division colors.

The parent palette is:

- Obsidian `#07090D`
- Carbon `#0D1016`
- Porcelain `#F4F6F8`
- Steel `#919AA8`

The six division colors can appear together only as a spectrum/accent system, not as a permanent gradient inside the parent logo.

### Wordmark

Until a custom wordmark is drawn, use **SYPHER** in uppercase with generous tracking. Do not stylize individual letters in random ways. The symbol carries the distinctive geometry; the temporary wordmark should remain restrained.

## 5. Division identities

### Sypher Labs

- Signature: `#8C6BFF`
- Mark: **Specimen Frame**
- Geometry: open frame, sample core, measurement ticks.
- Visual behavior: grids, annotations, coordinates, incomplete diagrams, experimental states.
- Avoid: making Labs look like a generic “science atom” logo.

### Sypher Intelligence

- Signature: `#43D7FF`
- Mark: **Core Lens**
- Geometry: broken concentric field around a central diamond.
- Visual behavior: controlled luminosity, focus, context layers, calm movement.
- Avoid: generic stars, brains and assistant chat bubbles.

### Sypher Products

- Signature: `#38D6A4`
- Mark: **Module Field**
- Geometry: composed interface modules around negative space.
- Visual behavior: clean surfaces, product screenshots, whitespace, modular information.
- Avoid: overly futuristic UI that makes normal software feel inaccessible.

### Sypher Systems

- Signature: `#3F73FF`
- Mark: **Signal Rail**
- Geometry: synchronized rails crossing a stable center.
- Visual behavior: telemetry, data density, status signals, time-series language.
- Avoid: factory clichés, gears and generic dashboard iconography as the main identity.

### Sypher Engineering

- Signature: `#E99A55`
- Mark: **Interlock**
- Geometry: two structural paths mechanically crossing around a core.
- Visual behavior: material photography, exploded diagrams, measurements, hardware surfaces.
- Avoid: turning the entire division into an “industrial orange” construction brand.

### Sypher Interactive

- Signature: `#E05268`
- Mark: **Echo Gate**
- Geometry: nested echo brackets resolving into a beat/core.
- Visual behavior: more expressive typography, authored art, motion, atmosphere and sound/rhythm cues.
- Avoid: forcing game artwork into the same corporate card system used by Products or Systems.

## 6. Product identity rule

Products do **not** reuse the division logo with a different name. Each product earns a standalone mark and can be much more expressive than the parent company.

The relationship is shown through endorsement:

- `Illume — Sypher Intelligence`
- `Nodi — Sypher Products`
- `SincroHub — Sypher Systems`
- `Manopla Inteligente — Sypher Engineering`
- `Réquiem — Sypher Interactive`

For marketing copy, “by Sypher” is acceptable when the division would add clutter. For technical or portfolio contexts, prefer the full division lineage.

### Current project marks

- **Illume:** the existing approved reactor/core artwork remains the master identity. The SVG in this repository is a web companion reconstruction, not a replacement for the original master asset.
- **Nodi:** Node-N — connected points resolve into the product initial and project-network idea.
- **SincroHub:** Sync Orbit — synchronized arcs around a stable operational hub.
- **Manopla:** Brace Core — a structural wearable/physical-interface frame around a core.
- **Réquiem:** Bell Echo — vertical signal, bell/echo geometry and aged-gold terminal detail.

## 7. Typography

The implementation uses a neutral grotesk/system sans while the identity is still moving. The hierarchy is more important than a prematurely purchased font:

- Display: tight tracking, large scale, medium weight.
- Body/UI: neutral sans, high legibility.
- Technical labels: monospace or uppercase micro-labels with wide tracking.

Interactive can later adopt an authored display face for game presentation while keeping corporate labels consistent.

## 8. Tone of voice

Sypher should sound like a company that builds, tests and learns — not a futuristic corporation pretending to already be enormous.

Prefer:

- concrete capabilities;
- current status;
- engineering language when useful;
- restrained ambition;
- “in formation”, “prototype”, “MVP”, “pre-production” where accurate.

Avoid:

- “revolutionary” without evidence;
- fake enterprise metrics;
- fabricated clients/partners;
- language implying a large team or mature corporation when that is not true.

## 9. Founder and After Hours

The founder identity is connected to Sypher but is not a division. Personal art, streaming, YouTube and creator experiments belong under **After Hours**, explicitly labeled as personal and non-company work.

This protects both sides:

- the company can remain credible and focused;
- the founder can show personality, hobbies and creator work without pretending they are corporate products.

## 10. What is locked vs. still open

### Locked enough to build with

- project lineage;
- division color families;
- dark-first geometric grammar;
- endorsement model;
- separation between company and personal After Hours work.

### Still open

- final custom Sypher wordmark;
- final master vector for Illume;
