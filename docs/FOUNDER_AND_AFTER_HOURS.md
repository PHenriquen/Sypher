# Founder and After Hours

Sypher can acknowledge the founder's broader creative identity without treating personal hobbies as company output.

## Founder links

The corporate site may link to the founder's professional presence:

- personal portfolio;
- GitHub;
- LinkedIn.

These links belong on the Founder page and in the footer. They are not Sypher products.

## After Hours

`After Hours` is a personal, non-company area attached to the Founder profile. It can contain:

- drawings and visual studies;
- Twitch / streaming;
- YouTube and TikTok creator experiments;
- personal game/media hobbies;
- other creative experiments.

Every After Hours surface must make the boundary explicit: this content is personal and is not a Sypher division, product, client engagement or R&D program.

## Social-network rule

Do not create a corporate-looking directory of every social account. Add a social link only when it has a stable public role.

Professional links belong to Founder. Creator links belong to After Hours. General-purpose social feeds such as X/Twitter should stay out until they have a deliberate public role.
